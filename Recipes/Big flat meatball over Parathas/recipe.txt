#Strudel is a sweet preparation from Austria, often filled with apples. This variant uses cabbage as filling.
#2 servings
*Flat meatballs
- Get [280g_ of mixed _beef and pork] (2/3 <beef>, 1/3 <pork>)
- Add <Salt>, <Pepper>, <Garlic powder>, and a bit of <Mace>
- Mix, form a ball, and let it rest in the fridge for at least 2 hours
- Add [2 tablespoons_ of _breadcrumbs], and [2_ _eggs]
- Mix for at least 2 mins, all the <eggs> should be incorporated and the mix should become a bit elastic
- Chop [30g_ of _cheese] (dry mozzarella for me) in small cubes and mix with the meat
- Divide into [2] portions and create [2] disks of about {3 cm} tall
- In a flat pan, add a bit of <oil> and bring it to high temperature
- Place the patties and let them cook 3 minutes from one side. After the first minute, cover with a lid
- After 3 minutes, drip a bit of oil on the top, turn and continue cooking the other side for another 3 minutes
- Turn off the heat, and let them stay 5 mins with the lid on
*Paratha
- Get [200g_ of _flour], mix it with [5g_ of _salt], add [110g_ of warm _water] and mix
- Add [5g_ of _oil] and continue mixing for 2 minutes
- Let it rest 20 minutes
- Roll them very thin and long, then brush some melted <ghee> (clarified butter) over
- Fold together the dough on the short side, create a loose string, then roll it in a spiral
- Compact it well, then flatten it with your hands and cover with plastic. Then add a weight over it and keep it for at least 30 mins
- Heat a pan with some ghee and cook the parathas until fully cooked through (about 4 mins per side), change the cooking side at least a couple of times
*Sweet potato chips
- Get [1_ large _sweet potato], possibly cylindrical
- Wash it well, keep the skin, and slice it over a mandolin
- Put the slices on a plate and sprinkle a bit of <water>. Microwave at 750w for 1 minute
- Place them on a kitchen towel, without overlapping, and let them dry out for at least 1 hour
- Get a pan with {1cm} of frying oil, bring it to {180°C} and fry them for about 4 minutes
- Drain and sprinkle with only <salt>
*Sauce
- Mix [1 tablespoons_ of _Mayo] with [20g_ of _cream cheese] and [1 teaspoon_ of _barbecue sauce], add [1 pinch_ of _dried fenugreek leaves]. Mix well
- Sprinkle some <dried fenugreek leaves> (<Kasoori methi>) over the Parathas
- Then add the meatball patties over, cover with chopped lettuce and add the sauce
