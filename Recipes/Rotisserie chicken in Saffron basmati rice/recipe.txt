#Basmati rice is cooked and flavored with saffron, a baking pot is filled with shredded rotisserie chicken flavored with sundried tomatoes and caramelized onions
#6 servings
*Filling
- Get [1_ _rotisserie chicken]
- Remove all the bones and shred the meat a bit; keep the skin
- Get [4_ _onions], and slice them not too fine (I used a mix of white and red onions)
- Add [30g_ of _butter] in a pan and melt it
- Add the <onions> on high heat; let them release the moisture
- Prepare a <cartouche>: fold a piece of parchment paper always on the same vertex, to have a triangle, then cut the outside to have it as a circle that can cover your pan
- Once the liquid is removed, reduce heat to a minimum and cover them with a cartouche
- Caramelize them slowly, stirring from time to time
- Once done, add salt and deglaze with <vinegar>
- Chop [10_ _sun-dried tomatoes]
- Mix together <onions>, <tomatoes>, and <chicken>
*Rice
- Wash one time [500g_ of _basmati rice]
- Cover with <water>, bring to a boil, then cook it covered very gently for the expected time
- Remove from heat and keep it covered for 10 minutes before removing the lid, then mix it and let it cool down
- Get [20ml_ of hot _water] and dissolve inside <Saffron> ([5g_ of _saffron] in packets), add [4_ egg _yolks], and mix well
- Add the <saffron> and <yolks> mix to the cold <rice> and mix well. If it is too fluid, add [1 tablespoon_ of _rice flour]
=Composing
- Get a medium pot that can go in the oven, like a Dutch oven, {1.5 liters} capacity should be perfect
- Grease with <butter> or <lard>
- Put 1/3 of the rice on the bottom and 1/3 on the border, and press it well
- Fill the center with the mixture of chicken, onions, and dry tomatoes
- Cover with the remaining rice
- Place in the oven for 20 minutes at {180°C}
- Let it cool down 15 minutes, then turn it, slice, and serve
- The decoration topping was chopped parsley mixed with chopped sun-dried tomatoes and chopped cured green peppers
