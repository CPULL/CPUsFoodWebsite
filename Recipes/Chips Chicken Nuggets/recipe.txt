#Chicken meat blended with bread and spices, then coated with crumbled potato chips
#chicken, bread, salt, pepper, garlic powder, onion powder, sweet paprika, mustard, oil, semolina flour, AP flour, sparkling water, potato chips, frying oil, zucchini, olive oil, fresh mint, white vinegar
# 4 servings
*Chicken Nuggets
- Get [750 g_ of _chicken] (breast and thighs); have it fridge cold
- Chop it into big pieces and add it to a food processor
- Get a slice of [200 g_ of _bread], remove the crust, cube it, and add it to the food processor
- Season with [5 g_ of _salt], [5 g_ of _pepper], [1 teaspoon_ of _garlic powder], [1 teaspoon_ of _onion powder], [1 tablespoon_ of _sweet paprika], and [3 teaspoons_ of _mustard]
- Add [1 splash_ of _milk], [2 tablespoons_ of _oil]
- Mix until smooth; keep it cold in the fridge
- Wet your hands with <water>, get a small handful of mixture, form a flat ball, and place it into a dish; avoid placing one over another
- Repeat until all the mix is consumed. Keep them in the fridge
+Breading
- In a dish add [100 g_ of _semolina flour]
- In another dish, mix slowly [100 g_ of _AP flour] with [180 ml_ of _sparkling water] using a whisk; add the water slowly to the flour to avoid lumps
- Get [2 bags_ of _potato chips] and crush them with your hands; if you prefer them very fine, use a meat mallet. Add them to a 3rd dish
- Get the cold nuggets prepared; pass them first in <semolina>, then in the coating mixture, and finally cover with crushed potato chips
+Frying
- Heat [1 L_ of _frying oil] to {170°C}
- Place 4 or 5 nuggets at a time inside (depends on how big your pot is; do not overlap them)
- Let them cook at {170°C} for 5 minutes, then place them on paper to remove the excess oil
*Zucchini scapece
- Get [6_ _zucchini] and slice them as small disks; they should be {1 cm} tall
- Add salt over them and let them stay 10 minutes, then remove the water and drain with paper towels
- Cook in a pan with [4 tablespoons_ of _olive oil] for about 15 minutes
- Add [4 stems_ of _fresh mint], stir, and let it cook for 1 minute
- Remove from the pan and add [1 tablespoon_ of _white vinegar]; stir
+Serving
- Serve with your favorite dipping sauces
