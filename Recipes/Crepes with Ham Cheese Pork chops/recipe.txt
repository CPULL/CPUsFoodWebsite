#Classical French Crêpes, filled with 3 ingredientes: melting cheese, prosciutto, and pork chops; each filling on a different fold to create multiple layers
#salt, garlic powder, allspice, ground cumin, dry cilantro, pepper, thyme, fresh pork ham, water, eggs, butter, milk, AP flour, cheese, prosciutto
#3 servings
*Pork chops
- In a cup, add [2 teaspoons_ of _salt], [1 teaspoon_ of _garlic powder], [0.5 teaspoon_ of _allspice], [0.5 teaspoons_ of _ground cumin], [1 teaspoon_ of _dry cilantro], [5 g_ of _black pepper], and [1 teaspoon_ of _thyme]
- Get [6_ thin slices of fresh _pork ham] (<pork hock> or <pork shank>), spread the spice mixture over them on both sides, stack them, and let them marinate for a couple of hours
- In a pan heat a splash of oil and cook on high flame the slices for 2 minutes per side
- After cooking the meat, add [0.5 glass_ of _water] in the pan to deglaze; keep this liquid for later
*Crepes
- In a big bowl add [1 pinch_ of _salt] and [3_ _eggs]; whisk them a little
- Add [40 g_ of liquid _butter] (just barely warm) and mix again
- Add [500 ml_ of whole _milk] and whisk again
- Pass [250 g_ of _AP flour] through a sieve into the bowl and wish to incorporate the flour; do it in 2 or 3 times to avoid lumps (a few lumps here are fine)
- Do not mix too much, cover with plastic, and let it rest in the fridge for at least 1 hour
- Add {3 tablespoons_} of the rested mixture to the deglazed pan, heat, and mix with a spatula until a gravy will form
- Place a crepe pan (or a very flat pan) on medium heat. It should be {22cm} diameter
- For each crepe, add a small knob of <butter> and let it melt, then spread it over the pan surface
- Add a big ladle of the crepe mixture at the center of the pan, and move the pan to have it cover the entire surface
- Cook for about one and a half minutes per side, then flip it with the help of a spatula and let the crepe cook for another minute and half
- Stack the crepes one over another to keep them moist
=Assembly
- This recipe should produce {6_} crepes
- Prepare [12 slices_ of melting _cheese] (I used <scamorza>) and [6 slices_ of _prosciutto]
- Place one crepe in a dish
- On the bottom left corner add 2 slices of <cheese>
- On the bottom right corner add 1 slice of <prosciutto>
- Fold the crepe vertically in half
- Add one slice of pork chop over half of the folded crepe, and fold the crepe over it
- Place the folded crepes in a baking tray covered with parchment paper
- Place the tray in a preheated oven at {180°C} for 5 minutes to have the cheese melt and the pork chops reheat.
- Serve with the gravy on top and your favorite side dish (sweet green peppers cooked with tomatoes for me)
