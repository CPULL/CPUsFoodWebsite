#This recipe was created to use rice and chicken leftovers
#3 servings
- This recipe uses leftovers from another video I did: https://www.youtube.com/shorts/Uw8g40yFah8
*Saffron Rice
- You need about [200g_ of cooked saffron _rice]
- If you do not have leftovers, boil {150g} of <basmati rice> with {180g} of <water>, then add <salt>, <white pepper>, <butter>, and <saffron>, and let it cool down a few hours
*Rotisserie Chicken
- You need about [200g_ of leftover _rotisserie chicken] mixed with <sundried tomatoes> and <caramelized onion>
- If you do not have it, you can make it with {150g} of <rotisserie chicken>, {2} chopped <sundried tomatoes>, and {1} <caramelized onion>
- Mix all together and let it rest a few hours
*Caramelized onions
- Get [2_ big _onions], and chop them not too fine
- Cook them in <butter> with a pinch of <salt> and <sugar> with a <cartouche> until they are brown
*Béchamel
- Get [30g_ of _butter] and [40g_ of _flour] with a pinch of <salt>, and mix them in a pan over medium heat until you form a <roux>
- Add [300ml_ of cold _milk] and stir over the flame until it thickens
- Add <pepper> and <nutmeg>
*Preparation
- Combine the <onions> with the remaining <chicken>, then incorporate a splash of <white wine> and a small piece of <butter>. Warm it up in a pan for 5 minutes at medium heat
- Mix the <rice> with the warm <béchamel>
- Add the <rice> on the bottom of a plate and place the <chicken> over
