#A different sauce for pasta, done with ground beef and without tomatoes
#lard, ground beef, salt, pepper, pasta water, pistachios
#4 portions
- Get [30g_ of _lard] as a block, chop in {1cm} pieces, and put it (without oil) in a pan at medium temperature
- Let it render for 15 minutes, turning it from time to time
- Make the pan very hot and add [500g_ of _ground beef]
- Break the meat in small chunks, be sure the water will evaporate
- When the meat is browned, add [1 teaspoon_ of _salt] and [1 teaspoon_ of _pepper]
- Cook the meat for 15 minutes in total
- Chop [1_ big white _onion] in chunks
- Chop roughly {1_} bunch of parsley ([30g_ of _parsley])
- Add <onion> and <parley> to a small food processor with a tablespoon of olive oil, blend until smooth
- Add the blend of onion and parsley to the meat at low temperature, cover and let it cook 5 minutes at very low temperature, mixing from time to time
- When serving, add {1 ladle_} of <pasta water>
- Top your pasta with [30g_ of _pistachios]
- I used mixed pasta for this dish
