#The eggplant/aubergine is partially cut and then cooked, the cuts are then filled with different ingredients. In my version, slices of tomatoes, mozzarella, and flat meatballs
#eggplants, salt, ground beef, egg, breadcrumbs, Roma tomatoes, sweet potato, red onions, beefsteak tomato, mirin, soy sauce, sugar, MSG, molasses, sesame seeds, oil
#2 servings
*Eggplants
- Get [2 long_ _eggplants], wash them, and cut the top part
- Place 2 wooden spoons (of equal size) on the sides of the eggplant and with a knife cut slices of {2cm}. Use the spoons to prevent cutting through the eggplants completely
- Brush the eggplants with [1 teaspoon_ of _salt], also inside the cuts
- Brush some <olive oil> around the eggplant, including the cuts
- Preheat an oven at {190°C} and place the eggplants inside over parchment paper. Let them cook for 30 minutes, then let them cool down completely
- Get [150g_ of _ground beef], add [1_ _egg], and [3 tablespoons_ of _breadcrumbs]
- Season with <Salt>, <Pepper>, <Garlic powder>, <Thyme>, <MSG>
- Mix and divide into small portions (about {15g} each)
- In a plate add <breadcrumbs>, press the mix, and create flat discs; be sure to have breadcrumbs on both sides
- Add some <olive oil> in a pan, and add the meatball discs. Cook 3 minutes per side
- Slice [3_ _Roma tomatoes] as thin disks, salt the slices
- Chop some <mozzarella> in thin slices and cut them same size as the <tomatoes> and <meatballs>
- Fill the cuts of the now cold eggplants, alternating meatballs, tomatoes, and mozzarella
- Place them in the oven, not preheated, at {160°C} for 5 minutes
- Use the parchment paper to move the eggplants to a dish
*Candied sweet potatoes (Daigaku-imo)
- Wash well [1 big_ _sweet potato], and cut it into bite-size pieces, keeping the skin
- Boil in <salted water> for 5 minutes, drain, and let it cool down
- Chop [2 small_ _red onions]
- Chop also the <green stems>
- Chop [0.5_ _beefsteak tomato] into small dice
- In a bowl mix [5g_ of _salt] with [30g_ of _mirin] and [30g_ of _soy sauce]
- Add [2 tablespoons_ of _sugar] and [2g_ of _MSG]
- Add [3 tablespoons_ of _molasses] and [1 tablespoon_ of _sesame seeds] and mix well
- In a pan add [2 tablespoons_ of _oil] and have it on high heat
- Add the chopped <sweet potatoes>, add the chopped <onions>, and cook for 1 minute
- Add the liquid mixture and cook on high flame until the liquid thickens (about 5 minutes)
=Side dish
- Stack the the remaining <Roma tomato>es, <meatball>s, and <mozzarella>, and add a small <basil> leaf. Use half toothpicks to keep them stacked
