#Tomatokeftedes are Greek fritters of tomato and feta
#tomatoes, red onions, Feta, dill, flour, baking powder, bell pepper, shallot, chickpeas, breadcrumbs, egg, cucumber, garlic powder, salt, parsley, lemon, Greek yogurt, olive oil
#3 servings
- Get [500g_ of _tomatoes], clean them, remove the seeds and the liquid, and finely chop the pulp
- Chop finely [2_ _red onions] and add them to the <tomatoes>
- Get [200g_ of _Feta] and crumble it in the tomatoes
- Chop [1 bunch_ of _parsley] and [1 bunch_ of _dill]
- Do not forget <salt> and <pepper>
- Finish with [4 tablespoons_ of _flour] and mix well; it should be soft but not liquid. Add more <flour> if needed
- Add [10g_ of _baking powder]
- Add about {1 cm} of frying <oil> to a pan
- With a couple of spoons, spoon a spoon in the oil and make it flat (about {2 cm}). [Yes, the pun was intended.]
- Cook 4 minutes per side at {170°C}
*Chickpea and pepper koftes
- Get [1_ red _bell pepper], clean it, and chop it into small chunks
- Chop [1_ _shallot] and cook it and the pepper in a pan with a bit of <olive oil> for about 15 minutes
- Get [1 can_ of _cooked chickpeas] and blend them with an immersion blender together with the cooked <bell pepper> and [1 clove_ of _garlic] and some basil
- If it is too thick to blend, add [1 tablespoon] of the governing water from the chickpeas (<aquafaba>)
- Add [5 tablespoons_ of _breadcrumbs] and [1_ _egg] and mix with a spoon; if it is too liquid, add more <breadcrumbs>
- Form balls and place them on a baking tray with parchment paper; press the balls to make them flat (about {2cm} tall)
- Add a small piece of melting <cheese> on top
- Bake at {180°C} for 18 minutes; spray some <water> in the oven while cooking
*Tzatziki
- Remove the skin from [0.5_ _cucumber], and grate it
- Add [1 teaspoon_ of _garlic powder] or [1_ crushed _garlic clove]
- Add [1 pinch_ of _salt] and [20g_ of minced _parsley]
- Add the zest and the juice of [1_ _lemon]
- Add to the bowl [250g_ of _Greek yogurt]
- Add [1 tablespoon_ of _olive oil] and mix well
